### 💻 About Me

I am a final-year Information Technology student at the University of Calcutta with a prior degree in Physics. I focus on building systems where performance, data integrity, and architecture matter—ranging from in-browser virtual runtimes and distributed geospatial platforms to enterprise Spring Boot backend services.

Having a physics background means I approach software from first principles: isolate variables, model the edge cases, test failure modes, and keep abstractions minimal.
